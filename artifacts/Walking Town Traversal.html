Walking Town Traversal

Five ways to move a town on four legs between battles. All five run the same loop: scan, pick a site, walk or send the team by bike, fight a stand-in battle, haul the fuel home. Each one was played start to finish by an automated harness, which measured how long and how many taps it takes to reach a battle from the start.

Recommendation

Build on Isometric Expanse, with the Route Map as its zoomed-out radar view. Both reach a battle in 5 seconds and 2 taps, so traversal stays a short beat and doesn't overshadow the fights. Keep the Horizon Strip only as a short side-on shot while the town walks. Keep 3D on hold until there is an art budget for it, and cut Hex.
